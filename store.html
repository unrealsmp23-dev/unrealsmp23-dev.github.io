Licence keys are made by hand, so after you pay, send a message on Discord to get yours.

1

Pay with PayPal

Pay with PayPal using the Buy button for the plan you want. Keep your receipt.

2

Message The_Shadow333 on Discord

Send your PayPal receipt and the name you want on the licence. For Basic, also send your Server ID. To find it, start your server once with the plugin installed and no licence, and it shows in the console:

Server ID: 7F3K9QX2  (send this when buying a Basic license)

Add your key

Put your name and key into plugins/UnrealEssentials/license.yml, then restart the server.

What's inside

Each one is a separate module, so you only switch on the ones your server needs.

Moderation

Admin panel
Kick, ban and mute with reasons, and view inventories and ender chests, all from a menu. /admin
Invsee
View and edit inventories live. /invsee /ecsee
Mutes
Mute players or lock all chat. /mute /mutechat
Silent punish
Kick or ban with no leave message. /skick /sban
Action log
Searchable history of what staff did. /actionlog
Chat filter
Censor or block words in chat and on signs. /filter
Command blocker
Block and hide commands like /pl and /version.
Mod checker
Detect banned mods and hacked clients. /modcheck
Server lock
Let in only staff and a bypass list. /lock

Events and content

Orbital strikes
Custom strike patterns, special weapons and pearl cannons. /orbital
NPCs
Survival NPCs with skins, dialogue and PvP. /npc
Marches
NPC armies marching in formation along a path. /march
Replay
Record players and play them back as NPCs. /replay
Recording
Session timer and event log for filming. /session /rec
Teleprompter
Lines for actors on the action bar, reacting to Simple Voice Chat. /prompter
Queues
Join at an NPC, get let onto the stage one at a time. /queue
Teams
Coloured teams with a menu, shuffle and team chat. /uteam
Fake borders
Per player world borders that can shrink. /fakeborder

Survival

Half heart
Hits that would kill leave players on half a heart.
Death actions
Spectator, fake leave, kick or ban on death. /revive
Inventory rollback
Snapshots on death and quit, with a restore menu. /rollback
Dimension lock
Lock the Nether, the End or any world. /dimlock
Kits
Item kits with cooldowns. /kit /createkit
Custom items
Turn the item in your hand into a custom one. /citem
Custom villagers
Traders with trades you set in a menu. /cvillager
Warps and spawn
Named warps, a server spawn and a 50 slot teleport menu. /warp /spawn

Everyday commands

Nicknames and skins
Set them separately, or open a menu. /nick /skin
Player tools
Vanish, heal, feed, gamemodes, hunger, health and keep inventory.
Teleports
World, top, offline player and dimension teleports. /top /otp
Items
Repair, rename and random heads. /repair /rename
Chat tools
Clear chat, broadcast, and join, leave and death message range.
Server tools
Max players, auto item clear, rail speed and more.
Voice chat
Voice mutes and a global voice lock for Simple Voice Chat. /vmute
Control panel
One menu for every feature. /panel
