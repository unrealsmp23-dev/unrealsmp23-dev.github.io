Unreal SMP is a hardcore, story-driven Minecraft server. A cast of players acts out an ongoing story through scripted events, and if you die, you're out.

How it works

Hardcore means hardcore

This isn't a survival grind. Every session follows the story, and the story only works if the stakes are real. One death and your character's arc is over.

  • One lifeDie and you're removed from the story. There's no respawning back in.
  • ScriptedThe admins plan the big moments. Cast members play their characters through them.
  • On cameraSessions are recorded for content, so stay in character while you're on.
  • CommunityTeamwork and following the event plan matter more than winning.

The plugin that runs the show. It's built in-house for scripted and lore servers, and it's being tested on Unreal SMP before release.

/orbital

Orbital strikes

Cast a rod, reel it in, and a nuke lands on the bobber. Every strike is fully customisable.

/npc

Survival NPCs

Players with any skin who fight with swords, maces and spears, take knockback and die like real players.

/replay

Motion capture

Record actors and play them back as NPCs. Merge takes so one person can play a whole scene.

/prompter

Teleprompter

Lines on the action bar that turn green when the actor speaks in voice chat.

/session

Recording mode

Locks joins, sets the MOTD, mutes chat and logs every moment with timestamps for editing.

/admin

Staff tools

An admin panel, vanish, freeze, silent bans, a mod checker and inventory rollback.
